3 Golden Rules for Digital Safety

1 Never Share OTP or PIN

Bank executives, electricity officials, and police will never ask for your confidential OTP, CVV, or UPI PIN.

2 Verify Domain Extension

Authentic Indian banks use verified domains like .sbi or .bankofbaroda.in, not .top or .xyz.

3 Call 1930 within the Golden Hour

If money was mistakenly transferred, immediately call 1930 to freeze the fraud account before the scammer withdraws funds.
